**Fix: a refine caps what one turn adds or rewrites, not the board's length (vivijure-core#473).**

`buildRefinementSystemPrompt` told the model the scenes array may hold at most `PLANNER_ASK_MAX_SCENES` (50) entries, and never to let it exceed that. A refine of a board over 50 scenes could therefore come back shorter than the board it was given, and a board could never grow past 50 by refining, against the stated plan that a long board is built over refinement turns. The refine prompt now says to return every scene it was given, in order, unless the user asks to remove some; to add or rewrite at most `PLANNER_ASK_MAX_SCENES` scenes in one turn and keep the rest for a later turn; and that the whole board may hold up to `STORYBOARD_MAX_SCENES` (600). The planning prompt is unchanged, and `PLANNER_ASK_MAX_SCENES` stays 50 (vivijure-cf's panel guard is held equal to it). Not observed live: this is a prompt change, and no model call was made.
