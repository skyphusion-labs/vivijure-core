**Fix: a film whose poll is cut off no longer stalls for five minutes, and the sweep drives a keyframes-only film nobody polls (vivijure-cf#1139).**

A film tick releases the advance lease in its `finally`. A Worker's execution context ends when its client disconnects, and the tick's pending awaits end with it, so that `finally` never ran and the lease stayed held for its full 300 s. A client that timed out each poll made a film advance one step per five minutes. `FILM_ADVANCE_LEASE_TTL_SECONDS` is now 60, and `holdFilmAdvance` renews the lease every `FILM_ADVANCE_LEASE_RENEW_SECONDS` (20) while the tick runs. A tick its host drops stops renewing and frees the film within 60 s. A tick that runs long keeps the film, so two drivers still never overlap. `advanceFilmJob` and `retryFilmFinish` both hold this way. New exports: `renewFilmAdvance`, `holdFilmAdvance`, `FILM_ADVANCE_LEASE_RENEW_SECONDS`, `FilmAdvanceHold`.

Sweep pass 1 (`listUnresolvedNotifiableJobs`, `countUnresolvedNotifiableJobs`) no longer leaves out `mode = keyframes-only`. The sweep is the only driver of a film with no client, so a keyframes-only film nobody polled never reached `done`. `claimRenderNotify` still refuses those rows, so they send no email.
