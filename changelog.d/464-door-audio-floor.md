**Fix: a line on a driven shot is padded only to the floor its own door declares (vivijure-core#464, vivijure-cf#957).**

`normalizeStoredLineWav` padded every line on an audio-driven shot to 3s with zeros. That floor is Wan 2.6's alone: Wan takes driving audio of 3 to 30 seconds and bills by its own 5/10/15 s grid. InfiniteTalk and p-video size the clip to the audio, so a 1s line became a 3s clip, and they billed and animated about 2s of silence per shot. `MotionUsageDecl` gains an optional `driving_audio_min_seconds`. The core pads a line to it for a shot on that door only, and a door that declares none gets its line as bought. `parseMotionUsage` keeps a floor above 0 and at most 15s; `validateManifest` refuses a declared floor outside that range at load, because dropping it would hand the door a line it refuses. `normalizeLineWav(bytes, minSeconds = 0)` pads nothing by default. The 3s silence minted for an unlined shot on a door with no native audio is unchanged, and is now `UNLINED_SILENCE_SECONDS`. `LINE_WAV_MIN_SECONDS` stays exported as a deprecated alias of it. alibaba-wan declares 3 in vivijure-cf#1322. InfiniteTalk and p-video declare none: neither provider documents a minimum.
